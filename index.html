📖 How to Play

🎯 The Goal

Find the legendary 💎 Star of Africa hidden under one of the face-down tokens scattered across the continent.

Once found, race back to Cairo or Tangier to win. If someone else has the diamond, find a 🧲 Horseshoe or 🎫 Visa — arrive at a start city first to steal the win!

2–6 players · Ages 7+ · Based on Afrikan Tähti 1982

🚦 Setup

  • Each player starts in Cairo or Tangier with £300
  • 30 face-down tokens placed randomly across African cities
  • Highest die roll goes first; play clockwise
  • You can name your player before starting

🗺️ Moving

  • Roll the die — move up to that many steps
  • You may stop early at any city
  • Land routes (dotted lines) — free
  • Sea ⛵ (blue dashed) — costs £100 per voyage
  • Air ✈️ (red dashed) — costs £500 per flight
  • No money? Sea is free but limited to 2 steps

🪙 Flipping Tokens

When you land on a city with a token you have 3 choices:

  • Pay £100 — flip it now, guaranteed
  • Roll the die — 4, 5 or 6 flips it free!
  • Pass — skip it, your turn ends

💎 What You Can Find

  • 💎 Star of Africa — Race back to win!
  • Ruby — Collect £1,000
  • Emerald — Collect £600
  • Topaz — Collect £300
  • 🦹 Bandit — Lose ALL your money!
  • 🧲 Horseshoe / 🎫 Visa — Steal the win once diamond is found
  • ⬜ Blank — Nothing here

🌟 Special Cities

  • Cape Town ⭐ — First visitor earns £5,000 bonus!
  • Gold Coast 🥇 — All gems worth DOUBLE
  • Slave Coast — Blank token means skip your next turn
  • Sahara ☠️ — May get stuck; roll 1 or 2 to escape
  • St Helena ☠️ — Same hazard as Sahara
